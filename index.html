THE SHORT STORY

I’m a student developer who enjoys exploring how technology works — and, more importantly, how it can work better for people.

I learn by building. From experimenting with responsive websites to exploring Android app ideas and helping bring student tech events to life, I like taking a concept, breaking it into practical steps and turning it into something people can use.

I’m still growing as a developer, and that’s the point: every project is a chance to ask better questions, write better code and understand the problem more deeply.
